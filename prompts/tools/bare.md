## Tools available to you

- **KiCad 10 command line, `kicad-cli`**: ERC, DRC, netlist, Gerber, drill and position-file export, and renders. Run `kicad-cli --help` and `kicad-cli <cmd> --help` for usage.
- **KiCad 10 standard symbol, footprint and 3D libraries** in the system KiCad install.
- **Python 3** with the standard library. You may `pip install` general-purpose packages into a virtual environment.
- **Web access** for datasheets and the LCSC / JLCPCB parts catalog.

The KiCad GUI is not available. Everything has to be done through files and command-line tools.
